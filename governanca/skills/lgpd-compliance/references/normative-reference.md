## Lei 13.709/2018 (LGPD), consolidated text

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| Art. 4º, I | Does not apply to processing by a natural person for purely private, non-economic purposes |
| Art. 5º, I and II | Personal data; sensitive personal data (racial or ethnic origin, religious belief, political opinion, union or religious/philosophical/political organization membership, health or sex life, genetic or biometric data) |
| Art. 6º | Principles, including purpose (I), adequacy (II), necessity (III), transparency (VI), security (VII), prevention (VIII), accountability (X) |
| Art. 7º | **10 legal bases (I to X)** for common data: I consent · II legal/regulatory obligation · III public policies (public administration) · IV research body · V contract or pre-contract at the data subject's request · VI exercise of rights in proceedings · VII protection of life · VIII health protection by health professionals/authorities · IX legitimate interest · X credit protection |
| Art. 8º | Consent: written or other demonstrable means; burden of proof on the controller (§2º); revocable at any time by a free and facilitated procedure (§5º) |
| Art. 9º, §2º | Change of purpose under consent: inform the data subject beforehand |
| Art. 10 | Legitimate interest: concrete situations, strictly necessary data, transparency; ANPD may request a RIPD (§3º) |
| Art. 11, II | **7 hypotheses (a–g)** for sensitive data without consent; (g) = fraud prevention and security of the data subject in identification and authentication processes in electronic systems. Contract and legitimate interest are not among them |
| Art. 12 | Anonymized data is not personal data, unless the anonymization can be reversed with reasonable means |
| Art. 13, §4º | Definition of pseudonymization (data re-associable only with additional information kept separately) |
| Art. 14, §1º | Children's data: specific and highlighted consent from at least one parent or the legal guardian. Teenagers: caput (best interest) |
| Arts. 15 and 16 | End of processing; conservation allowed only for: I legal/regulatory obligation · II research body · III transfer to third party under the law · IV exclusive use of the controller, no third-party access, **and anonymized** |
| Art. 18 | **9 rights**: confirmation · access · correction · anonymization/blocking/deletion of unnecessary or excessive data · portability · deletion of data processed with consent · information on sharing · information on the possibility of refusing consent and its consequences · revocation of consent. Free of charge (§5º) |
| Art. 19 | Confirmation/access: **simplified format immediately (I)**; **clear and complete statement within 15 days from the request (II)** |
| Art. 20 | Right to request review of decisions taken solely by automated processing. Since Lei 13.853/2019 the law **does not require the review to be done by a natural person** (the §3º that required it was vetoed) |
| Art. 33 | International transfer: **9 hypotheses (I to IX)**; II has 4 forms (specific clauses, standard clauses, global corporate rules, seals/certificates/codes) |
| Art. 37 | Controller and processor keep a record of processing operations, especially under legitimate interest |
| Art. 38 | RIPD: the ANPD **may determine** that the controller prepare it (not automatic for every activity; recommended practice for high risk) |
| Art. 39 | Processor follows the controller's instructions |
| Art. 41 | Controller appoints an encarregado; identity and contact published, preferably on the website |
| Art. 46, §2º | Security measures from the conception phase of the product or service through its execution (privacy by design) |
| Art. 48, §1º | Incident communication "within a reasonable period, as defined by the national authority" (see Res. 15/2024) |
| Art. 52, II | Simple fine of **up to 2% of the revenue in Brazil** of the private legal entity, group or conglomerate in the last fiscal year, excluding taxes, **limited in total to R$ 50,000,000.00 per infraction** |

## ANPD resolutions

### Res. CD/ANPD nº 2/2022 — small processing agents (agentes de tratamento de pequeno porte)

- **Art. 3º**: no simplified regime for high-risk processing or above the revenue limit.
- **Art. 4º, high risk**: cumulatively at least **one general criterion** (large scale, or may significantly affect interests and fundamental rights) **and one specific criterion** (emerging or innovative technology; surveillance or control of publicly accessible areas; decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling; sensitive data or data of children, teenagers or the elderly).
- **Art. 11**: may skip appointing an encarregado, but must keep a communication channel with data subjects.
- **Art. 14, doubled deadlines**: I answering data subject requests (Art. 18 §§3º and 5º); II communicating incidents to the ANPD and to data subjects (wording given by Res. 15/2024, **without** the old exception for risk to physical or moral integrity); III the complete statement of Art. 19, II; IV deadlines to provide information, documents and records requested by the ANPD.
- **Art. 15**: the simplified statement of Art. 19, I may be given within 15 days.

Official text: https://www.in.gov.br/en/web/dou/-/resolucao-cd/anpd-n-2-de-27-de-janeiro-de-2022-376562019

### Res. CD/ANPD nº 15/2024 — security incident communication

- **Art. 5º**: communicate an incident that may **significantly affect interests and fundamental rights** of data subjects **and** involves at least one of: sensitive data; data of children, teenagers or the elderly; financial data; authentication data in systems; data protected by legal, judicial or professional secrecy; large-scale data.
- **Art. 6º**: communicate to the ANPD within **3 business days**, counted from when the controller **learned that the incident affected personal data** (§1º); the communication can be complemented within **20 business days** (§3º).
- **Art. 9º**: communicate to the affected data subjects within **3 business days**, same starting point, in clear language.
- **Art. 10**: record **every** incident, communicated or not, for **at least 5 years from the date of the record**.
- Small processing agents: doubled deadlines (Art. 6º §8º and Art. 9º §6º).

Official text: https://www.in.gov.br/en/web/dou/-/resolucao-cd/anpd-n-15-de-24-de-abril-de-2024-556243024

### Res. CD/ANPD nº 18/2024 — encarregado

- The encarregado may be a **natural person or a legal entity** (Art. 12).
- Identity and contact published **in a prominent, easily accessible place on the website**; without a website, by other means (Art. 9º, §3º).
- No registration, certification or specific professional training is required (Art. 14).
- Communication in Portuguese (Art. 13).

Official text: https://www.in.gov.br/en/web/dou/-/resolucao-cd/anpd-n-18-de-16-de-julho-de-2024-572632074

### Res. CD/ANPD nº 19/2024 — international transfer

- Approves the Brazilian **standard contractual clauses** (Annex II), to be adopted **in full and without alteration** (Art. 16 of the Regulation); only the indicated blanks may be filled.
- Deadline to incorporate them into existing contracts: 12 months from publication (DOU 23/08/2024), so **it ended on 23/08/2025**.

Official text: https://www.in.gov.br/en/web/dou/-/resolucao-cd/anpd-n-19-de-23-de-agosto-de-2024-580095396

## Lei 15.211/2025 — ECA Digital

- Published 17/09/2025; **in force since 17/03/2026** (Art. 41-A, added by Lei 15.352/2026).
- **Art. 9º, §1º**: reliable age verification, **self-declaration forbidden**.
- **Art. 20**: loot boxes forbidden in games aimed at or likely accessed by children and teenagers.
- **Art. 22**: profiling of children and teenagers for advertising forbidden.
- **Art. 24**: accounts of children and teenagers **up to 16 years old** must be linked to a legal guardian.
- **Art. 31**: providers with **more than 1,000,000 users** in this age range publish **semiannual** reports.
- **Art. 35, II**: fine of up to **10% of the economic group's revenue in Brazil**; without revenue, R$ 10.00 to R$ 1,000.00 per registered user; **limited in total to R$ 50,000,000.00 per infraction**.
- Supervising authority: ANPD (Decreto 12.622/2025, Art. 2º).

Official text: https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/lei/L15211.htm

## Lei 15.352/2026 — the ANPD as a regulatory agency

Conversion of MP 1.317/2025. Creates the **Agência Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD)**, a special-nature autarchy under the Ministry of Justice and Public Security, subject to the Regulatory Agencies Law (Lei 13.848/2019). It does not change the duties of controllers; it foresees a plan to review existing regulations, so check for newer resolutions.

Official text: https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2026/lei/L15352.htm
